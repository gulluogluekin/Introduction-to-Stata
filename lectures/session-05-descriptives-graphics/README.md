# Session 5: Descriptive analysis and hotel-price graphics

Date: 12 October 2026

Assessment: individual checkpoint 4 of 4, worth 10% of the overall course grade.

## Learning goals

- Match descriptive statistics to variable types and questions.
- Compare hotel prices across cities and star categories.
- Export a reproducible grouped table.
- Create distribution, group-comparison, and relationship graphs.
- Use titles, axes, units, and notes to make graphics interpretable.

## Graded checkpoint 4

Run `exercise.do` from the project root with `data/derived/hotel_panel.dta`, supplied by
the instructor or generated by the preparation and analysis scripts. No earlier exercise
output is required.

Allow about 20-25 minutes. The exercise transfers the lecture's methods to a different
question: how do the distribution of quoted prices, hotel stars, and guest ratings relate
within **Budapest**? Instead of repeating a city-mean table, histogram, or price-distance
plot, students export a star-category median/percentile table, a box plot, and a
price-rating scatter plot. The exercise points students to the relevant help files,
including how to read a box plot.

Use one common sample: Budapest Hotels with nonmissing nightly price, stars, and guest
rating, and a nightly price above zero and at most EUR 1,000. Counts describe price quotes,
not distinct hotels; multiple quotes can come from the same hotel. Interpret patterns as
descriptive associations, not effects of stars or ratings on prices.

Submit the completed do-file with short interpretations and these separate exercise outputs:

- `output/tables/session05_exercise_star_summary.csv`
- `output/figures/session05_exercise_star_box.png`
- `output/figures/session05_exercise_rating_scatter.png`

Both figures must communicate a specific comparison or relationship and identify the
sample, observational unit, and units of measurement.
