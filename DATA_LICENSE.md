## Hotel data

Source: Gábor Békés and Gábor Kézdi, *Data Analysis for Business, Economics, and Policy*
(Cambridge University Press, 2021).

- Dataset documentation: <https://gabors-data-analysis.com/datasets/hotels-vienna/> and
  <https://gabors-data-analysis.com/datasets/hotels-europe/>
- Source repository: <https://osf.io/r6uqb/>
- Raw Vienna download: <https://osf.io/g5dmw/download>
- Full European raw download used to create the course extract:
  <https://osf.io/yzntm/download>

The authors allow these data and their cleaning code to be shared, modified, and used for
educational purposes; they may not be used commercially. The source describes the hotel
records as anonymized and slightly altered to protect confidentiality.

Files covered by these terms:

- `data/raw/hotelbookingdata-vienna.csv`, an unchanged source download.
- `data/raw/hotelbookingdata-central-europe.csv`, an unmodified-row extract containing the
  Vienna, Budapest, and Zagreb observations from the full European raw file.
