## Exercise design

Each teaching-session exercise keeps the session's core concepts while changing a
meaningful part of the task. Students use help to adapt the syntax, make a comparison or
interpretation, and check the result. The session READMEs describe the contrast with the
lecture and the input files needed. Exercises save separate outputs, so a student's attempt
cannot replace the lecture example or another exercise's starting data.

## Assessment relationship

Session 1 is ungraded. The four individual checkpoints in Sessions 2–5 each contribute 10%
of the course score, making up the 40% checkpoint component. The final in-class exam in
Session 6 contributes the remaining 60%. The course is Pass/Fail with a total-score pass
threshold of 60. Detailed exam instructions will be provided by the instructor separately.

## Supplementary material

The existing files under `lectures/session-06-analysis-simulation/` cover regression,
factor variables, margins, and resampling. They remain available as optional supplementary
material, not as teaching scheduled for Session 6 or required exam preparation. Their
exercise varies factor coding and adjusted predictions, then resamples a median.
